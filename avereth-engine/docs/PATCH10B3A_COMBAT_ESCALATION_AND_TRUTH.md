# 10B-3A – Combat commitment, chronicle and creative Dungeon foes (.70)

Implemented on 24 October 2026, based on development HEAD `1991d6a` (product .69). The [reviewed plan](PATCH10B3_COMBAT_ESCALATION_AND_TRUTH_PLAN.md) remains the design/history source.

- **Commitment is separate from permission.** `mayOpenFight` retains its violence/causality rules. At the world-application gate, a narrated ultimatum, demand, aimed weapon or explicit waiting does not become First Strike just because the extractor emitted `hostile` or `intent:attack`. Supplied evidence must occur in the reply and is checked in its surrounding sentence. Real attacks, pronoun continuations, new ambushers and the existing group-identity rules remain supported. Extractor summaries and the injected narrator instruction clarify this boundary. Reports without prose retain their legacy semantics.
- **Use existing combat evidence.** Narrator and extractor receive a cumulative count of PC attack-skill uses reconstructed from the latest encounter's saved events, stopping at `encounter.started`. Overlapping snapshots/outcomes are deduplicated; the visible 20-line log is not the only source. No new event type, persistent counter, LLM call or shadow ledger. Incomplete history supplies no asserted total. Existing clause-level combat checks now also reject concrete excess cast/bolt-impact counts and excess skeleton remains during combat, across facts, memories, threads and quest notes. Casts and resolved strikes are distinct; multi-hit/area actions, neighboring flavor and attributed rumors remain possible.
- **Creative foes spend existing slots.** Current `ahead` retains priority. Without it, a present foe may bind one uniquely compatible free slot on the current floor/cycle, matching body plan and count. Early guards additionally need a guard/patrol scene; bosses retain normal pacing. Occupied/taken slots and peaceful visitors do not become new budget. Species/body-plan normalization occurs before matching. Existing `slotEntityEvents` supplies canonical identity, level, type and profile; `stronger:true` cannot upgrade it. A namespaced creative reference is remembered as optional `story_ref` on the existing `dungeon.slot` event, scoped to this floor/cycle, for repeat references and replay. No new spawn system or migration.
- **Keep seal and memory authority.** An early Warden neither resolves its living predecessor nor opens the next floor. Rejected foes produce a specific correction, including that imagined keys create no gate requirement; their active-foe/key clauses cannot enter the same reply's lasting stores. Existing object ownership, keys, passages, clearing, XP, loot and reset rules remain authoritative.

## Deliberate limits

This is a narrow contradiction gate, not a complete natural-language verifier or narrator rewriter. Unrecognized paraphrases can still require correction in a natural run; the generated prose is not guaranteed perfect. Incomplete old encounter history is not reconstructed by guessing. New `story_ref` metadata is optional; old events replay unchanged. No XP/balance, monster database, progression, recovery budgets, runner or `current_test` changes. ## Reviewfix .71 — positive commitment and actual presence

Based on `.70` / `f83114c`. Supersedes its permissive `attackCommitted` fallback, including reports with no prose: an immediate attack action must now have a positively identified actor in the reply. Existing names, target handles, creature/group aliases and local pronoun/coordination context establish who acts; another actor's attack, quoted/conditional threats, noun mentions of attacks and neutral prose cannot authorize combat. Unrecognized or ambiguous wording produces the existing correction without automatic combat. No extra model call or persistent conflict state; this remains a bounded language check, not a general semantic verifier.

A known `story_ref` resolves identity without re-entering an already present creature. An absent creature may be reintroduced in its last actual room (`left.at`, existing `at`, then its slot's original room); another room requires an existing entrance/arrival event. Thus explicit pursuit/party movement and a return to the actual room remain valid, without alias teleportation, extra spawn or redundant `scene.entered`. No new movement or persistence system.

## Run 9 encounter/action/data consistency (.72)

Without a clear crossing, no forced arrival.
